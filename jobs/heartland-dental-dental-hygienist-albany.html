Commonly Asked Questions

How much do Heartland Dental supported practices pay Hygienists?

Heartland Dental supported practices offer competitive hourly base pay, along with opportunities to earn more through daily and quarterly bonuses, paid time off, and comprehensive benefits. If you’re a dental hygienist looking to increase your income, there are proven ways to grow your earning potential—both now and over the course of your career.

Do Heartland Dental supported practices offer flexible schedules?

Many Heartland Dental supported offices offer part-time roles, 4-day workweeks, and temporary opportunities. In addition hygienists have access to HDflex, Heartland Dental’s innovative staffing solution that allows supported hygienists to pick up extra shifts at other supported offices.

Qualifications:

  • Current dental hygienist license
  • Excellent working knowledge of dentistry, dental hygiene procedures, dental patient screening and medical history documentation
  • CPR Certification

Preferred Experience

  • Experience using Velscope, Diagnodent, digital scanner, digital x-rays and electronic medical record systems
  • Desire to continue learning and grow clinical skills to meet needs of patients and provide preventative care and overall maintenance of patients’ dental health
  • Clinical needs as required by office
  • Completion of Tuberculosis (TB) testing prior to starting employment

As a clinical provider, you’ll be respected for your expertise and empowered to provide exceptional patient care. You’ll have access to the resources, training, and support to grow your skills and build strong partnerships with the supported doctors you work alongside.

You’ll retain full clinical autonomy, with access to hygiene mentors, the latest technology, and industry-leading continuing education at no cost to you.

Heartland Dental

Heartland Dental is the nation's largest dental support organization, providing non-clinical administrative support services to more than 3,200 supported doctors across 37 states and the District of Columbia in over 2,000 dental offices. Each Heartland Dental supported office is unique to the community and the patients they serve. Supported doctors are the leaders of their practice and retain clinical autonomy. All Heartland Dental supported doctors are united by a common goal: delivering the highest quality dental care and experiences to the communities they serve.
